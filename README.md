# Watch Culture Platform

A premium watch culture platform built for horological storytelling, visual exploration, and community engagement.

---

## Project Purpose
Watch Culture Platform is designed to elevate the appreciation of horology beyond traditional watch blogs and generic aggregators. The platform emphasizes:
- **Visual Storytelling**: Editorial content that treats timepieces as works of engineering and art.
- **Interactive Exploration**: Features like Watch Battles, Watch IQ, Worth It?, Random Watch discovery, and deep-dive specs.
- **Community & Collections**: Personal collections, community stories, and collector interactions.
- **Refined Aesthetics**: Timeless, elegant design crafted to meet the standards of watch-industry publications and events like the Ethos Watch Summit.
